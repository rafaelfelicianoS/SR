\section{AI Usage Statement}
\label{sec:ai}

Following the course AI-use policy, the team declares the use of
Claude (Anthropic) and Antigravity (Gemini) to generate the TikZ code of
the diagrams and the \LaTeX{} code of the tables, including the
requirements and threat tables, to brainstorm misuse cases, and to
review grammar, consistency and regulatory statements. Annex~C gives the
log. The case system, \mbox{R1--R9}, the design principles assessment,
the lifecycle plan, the \ac{CRA} analysis, and the threats and
requirements of the catalogue were defined and validated by the team.
The AI output was kept only after being checked, and edited or discarded
when it did not match the design or the sources. The authors reviewed
all the content and take full responsibility for it.

\section{AI Usage Statement}
\label{sec:ai}

Following the course AI-use policy, the team declares the use of
Claude (Anthropic) and Antigravity (Gemini) to generate the TikZ code of
the diagrams and the \LaTeX{} code of the tables, to brainstorm misuse
cases and propose a first draft of the threat table and of the
catalogue, and to review grammar, consistency and regulatory statements.
The case system, \mbox{R1--R9}, the design principles assessment, the
lifecycle plan and the \ac{CRA} analysis were defined by the team. The AI output was kept only after being checked,
and edited or discarded when it did not match the design or the sources.
The authors reviewed all the content and take full responsibility for it.

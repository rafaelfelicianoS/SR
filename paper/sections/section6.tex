\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented the secure planning of SecureBank~PT. The three
violations found in the design review were mitigated with short sessions
and step-up \ac{MFA}, isolation of internal services, and biometrics with
a six-digit \ac{PIN}. The lifecycle plan assigns an owner, tools and
evidence to each phase. Under the \ac{CRA}, the bank must already report
exploited vulnerabilities, and releases from December~2027 must meet
the essential requirements of \ac{CRA} Annex~I.

The \ac{CRA} (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847)~\cite{cra} introduces mandatory cybersecurity requirements for digital
products in the EU market. This section addresses the applicability of the \ac{CRA} to SecureBank PT, as it
identifies its core obligations, and establishes the necessary compliance timelines.

\subsection{Scope and Regulatory Boundaries}
Under Article~2, the \ac{CRA} applies to all software and hardware products whose intended purpose includes
logical or physical data connections. The SecureBank PT mobile banking application falls, therefore, in the
scope of the \ac{CRA}.

In contrast, the core banking backend, transaction databases and payment processing services are cloud and
financial entity infrastructures. As such, these operational environments are primarily governed by the
\ac{DORA}~\cite{dora} and the \ac{NIS2} Directives. While the \ac{DORA} regulates the operational resilience
and the risk towards the financial intitution, the \ac{CRA} regulates the software delivered directly to
consumer devices. Due to the fact that the application handles user authentication credentials, binding
financial transactions, it must satisfy rigorous conformity assessments proportional to high-risk consumer
software.


\subsubsection{Essential Requirements}
SecureBank PT is designed and delivered following the \emph{secure by default}:
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Access and Integrity:} the app rejects tampered or rooted environments and enforces
    validation of server identity through strict certificate pinning over TLS~1.3,

    \item \textbf{Data Protection and Attack Surface:} sensitive financial assets and personal data are
    encrypted in transit and at rest (R8). Debug interfaces and unnecessary third-party libraries are excluded
    from release builds to minimise the attack surface.
\end{itemize}


\subsubsection{Vulnerability Handling}
To ensure active vulnerability management throughout the product lifecycle, several measures were put in place.
Everytime a build pipeline is run, it automatically compiles and signs a machine-readable \ac{SBOM} in the CycloneDX
format, listing all third-party dependencies and SDKs to allow for vulnerability triaging.
The bank also publishes a Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD) policy via standardised \texttt{security.txt}
files. Security updates are issued promptly and separated from feature updates whenever possible. The software has a
guaranteed 5-year (minimum) security maintenace window.

\subsection{Obligations, Timelines, and National Supervision}
The \ac{CRA} sets binding milestones that dictate the deployment and operational schedule:

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Early Warning and Incident Reporting (11 September 2026):}
    Manufacturers must report any actively exploited vulnerability or severe security incident within $24$ hours of
    awareness to the competent national authority and \ac{ENISA}, followed by a detailed incident report within $72$
    hours.

    \item \textbf{Full Application (11 December 2027):}
    All essential requirements mentioned in the previous Section , assessment documentation, CE marking, and the EU
    declaration of conformity become legally enforceable for new releases.
\end{enumerate}

Within Portugal, the \ac{CNCS} serves as the designated national authority for market surveillance and
supervision under the \ac{CRA}. To ensure compliance with mandatory reporting obligations, the bank's \ac{PSIRT}
and Compliance Officer should established direct communication channels with the \ac{CNCS}.

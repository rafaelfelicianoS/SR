\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper presented the secure planning of SecureBank~PT. The three
violations found in the design review were mitigated with short sessions
and step-up \ac{MFA}, isolation of internal services, and biometrics with
a six-digit \ac{PIN}. The lifecycle plan assigns an owner, tools and
evidence to each phase. The security requirements catalogue refines
R1--R9 into 23 verifiable requirements, and the threat table shows that
every \ac{STRIDE} threat on the data-flow diagram is answered by at least
one of them. The security test plan gives every requirement at least
one of 21 test cases, combining a grey-box penetration test of five
attack types with ten fuzz vectors aimed at the parsers. A 15-minute
AFL++ run under sanitizers found the known faults of the old cJSON
release in under a second, and found nothing in the current release. Under the \ac{CRA}, the bank must already report
exploited vulnerabilities, and releases from December~2027 must meet
the essential requirements of \ac{CRA} Annex~I.

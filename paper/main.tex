\title{SecureBank PT: Secure Planning of a Mobile Online Banking Application}

\author{
\IEEEauthorblockN{Rafael Feliciano Soares, Guilherme Almiro Viana Fernandes Tavares, Francisco Antunes dos Reis,\\
Pedro Miguel Paulo Martins, Pedro Miguel dos Santos Marques}
\IEEEauthorblockA{\textit{Mestrado em Cibersegurança}, Universidade de Aveiro, Portugal\\
\{rafael, gavftavares, franciscoareis, pedropmartins, pmsmarques\}@ua.pt}
}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This paper presents the secure planning of SecureBank~PT, a mobile banking
application for a Portuguese retail bank that provides transfers, MB~WAY
payments and card controls. The plan follows the NIST Secure Software
Development Framework (SSDF) and uses OWASP SAMM~v2 to measure maturity.
A review of the initial design against ten secure design principles found
three violations: complete mediation, least common mechanism and
psychological acceptability. They are mitigated with short sessions and
step-up multi-factor authentication, isolation of internal services, and
biometrics with a six-digit PIN. For each lifecycle phase, the plan
defines the activities, owner, tools and evidence, and the security
requirements are refined into measurable non-functional requirements.
Finally, a Cyber Resilience Act quick-scan confirms that the application
is in scope and identifies the obligations that apply and their deadlines.
\end{abstract}

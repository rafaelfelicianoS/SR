\title{SecureBank PT: Secure Planning of a Mobile Online Banking Application}

\author{
\IEEEauthorblockN{Rafael Feliciano Soares, Guilherme Almiro Viana Fernandes Tavares, Francisco Antunes dos Reis,\\
Pedro Miguel Paulo Martins, Pedro Miguel dos Santos Marques}
\IEEEauthorblockA{\textit{Mestrado em Cibersegurança}, Universidade de Aveiro, Portugal\\
\{rafael.feliciano, gavftavares, franciscoareis, pedropmartins, pmsmarques\}@ua.pt}
}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
This paper presents the secure planning of SecureBank~PT, a mobile banking
application for a Portuguese retail bank that provides transfers, MB~WAY
payments and card controls. The goal is an application that is reliable,
robust and secure: it keeps serving customers under load and component
failures, rejects malformed, replayed and tampered requests, and protects
the money and personal data of its customers. The plan follows the NIST
Secure Software Development Framework (SSDF) and uses OWASP SAMM~v2 to
measure maturity.
A review of the initial design against ten secure design principles found
three violations: complete mediation, least common mechanism and
psychological acceptability. They are mitigated with short sessions and
step-up multi-factor authentication, isolation of internal services, and
biometrics with a six-digit PIN. For each lifecycle phase, the plan
defines the activities, owner, tools and evidence. A Cyber Resilience Act
quick-scan confirms that the application is in scope and identifies the
obligations that apply and their deadlines. Finally, the security
requirements are refined into a catalogue of 23 verifiable requirements,
each traced to a STRIDE threat on the data-flow diagram and to a
verification method.
\end{abstract}
